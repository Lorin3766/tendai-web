Your Data

Privacy & Security

Ask me profile questions automatically when I open Talk
On: Talk asks the next profile question, and a follow-up about a step you tried or something you already stated, from a fixed script on this device, without sending it to Google. Off: Talk won't ask profile questions or those follow-ups on its own; you can still fill in your profile via Edit profile data or ask Talk directly.

Follow-ups you turned off. Turn one back on to hear about that area again.

Run automatic health checks on new biometric data
On: newly logged steps or sleep are sent to Google's Gemini API to check for a significant shortfall. Off: nothing is sent; values still save on this device, and nothing checks them for shortfalls.
Generate advice on my Feed using AI
On: when the app or Feed opens and after a check-in, your age, goals, diet, habits and today's logged values are sent to Google's Gemini API to write the "Today's advice" card. Off: nothing is sent and the card is hidden.
Your data is stored on your device. To write Talk replies and Feed advice and run health checks, your messages and relevant profile and health data are sent to Google's Gemini API, sometimes automatically (when you open Talk or Feed, or when new steps or sleep are logged — each of these can be switched off above) — see the Privacy Policy. Obrium is not a medical service.
