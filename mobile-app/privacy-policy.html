Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

Obrium collects information you provide directly, including profile data, health information, and messages you send through the service.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide personalized wellbeing advice and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Your profile, health answers, logged metrics and chat history are stored locally on your device. To generate Talk replies and Feed advice and to run automatic health checks, parts of this data are sent through Obrium's relay server to Google's Gemini API, as described below. We do not retain chat history permanently.

AI Processing by Google Gemini

Obrium uses Google's Gemini API to write Talk's replies and Feed advice and to run automatic health checks. Each time Talk needs an AI response, Google receives the data below. Only fields you have actually provided are sent.

  • Conversation text: the message you send in Talk. Earlier messages in the chat are not included.
  • Profile: name, age, approximate location (city, ZIP code, or coordinates rounded to about 1 km), stated goals, diet and habits, sex at birth, gender identity, height, weight, body build and blood type.
  • Health and wellbeing answers: health conditions, medications, supplements, allergies, last checkup, stress, mental energy, anxiety, mood changes and sleep patterns.
  • Biometrics and daily context: today's steps and sleep, your energy check-in, and local weather.
  • App context: the latest status lines from your Focus Area cards, recent Obrium prompts, and notes Obrium has inferred about you from how you use the app (you can read these in Settings).

Feed advice is a separate, smaller request. It is sent automatically when the app opens or you open Feed, after a daily check-in, and when the connection to Obrium's relay is restored. It includes only what you have provided of: your age, goals, diet and habits, and today's steps, sleep, glasses of water and energy check-in. It does not include your name, location, messages, health answers or inferred notes.

Google processes this data under its own terms for the Gemini API.

Automatic Analysis

Some requests happen automatically, without you sending a message. When you open Talk while profile questions are still unanswered, Obrium asks Gemini to phrase the next question; this request includes your profile and the notes described above. When new steps or sleep values are logged (for example, when you mention them in Talk), Obrium sends only those values (date, steps, sleep) to Gemini to check for a significant shortfall. Both are on by default, and you can switch each off in Settings → Privacy & Security; when off, that request is not sent at all. Feed advice (described above) is also automatic and currently has no separate switch.

Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your information at any time through the Profile section of the app.
